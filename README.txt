THE LITTLE RED FLOWER CLUB: WEBSITE
Open index.html in Chrome or Edge. Click "Connect the flower" once and pick the ESP32 port.
No flower? Click "Enter without the flower" (bottom left), or press P.

KEYS     P place petal | R remove petal | D debug box
MEMO     Shift+E export posts | Shift+I import posts | Shift+X clear member posts (on the Memo Board page)
TESTING  add ?today=2026-11-17 to the Events address to test the date circle

FOLDERS  css/ js/ lib/ data/ fonts/ img/
  js/site-config.js   all settings (timing, sticker ID, skip link)
  js/site-gate.js     opening sequence + USB link
  js/site-flower3d.js the 3D flower on the opening page
  js/site-pages.js    menu, join form, events calendar, memo board
  data/flower-model.js your OBJ flower converted for the web
