ABOUT US

Who Are we?

The Little Red Flower Club is a non-for-profit social club that creates a safe space for people who are tired of living in society and its expectations.

Meet Our Mascots

Poppy

What do We do?
We run events that retrace back to childhood, a period of time where all that mattered was to be yourself. There are no networking session, no chasing deadlines, no pressure to be the best. Just be yourself, enhance your inner child.

We offer 3 main event categories: Bake & Chill for all yummy creations, Make & Play for all arts and craft, and Redo and Replay for your wildest creativity.
All events are free to join, just check out our events page for the latest updates!

Our Founder

Hello! my name is Mai, the founder of The Little Red Flower Club.

Growing up as an Chinese-Australian, cultural expectations has shaped how I think and behave: fight for the top and get rewarded a little red flower; but also lay low to avoid Tall Poppy Syndrome .I wanted a place to take a breather. That is why I created the Little Red Flower Club!

We will always welcome you with open arms :)

Mai
